Material Comparison
Material Typical Lifespan Best For Look
Asphalt Shingle 20–30 years Most home styles, budget-conscious re-roofs Wide range of colors and textures
Standing-Seam Metal 40–70 years Long-term owners, steep pitches, energy efficiency Clean vertical panel lines, several finish colors
Impact-Rated Shingle 20–30 years Hail-prone lots, possible insurance discount* Close to standard asphalt shingle

*Insurance discounts for impact-rated roofing vary by carrier and policy — ask your agent, or ask us and we'll point you in the right direction.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a roof replacement cost?

Cost depends on your roof's size, pitch, current condition, and the material you choose. We provide a written estimate after a free, in-person inspection — no guesswork over the phone.

Will you work directly with my insurance company?

Yes. If your roof has storm or wind damage, we can document it, meet with your adjuster, and help walk your claim through to approval.

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

Most residential re-roofs are completed in one to a few days depending on size, roof complexity, and weather. We'll give you a specific timeline as part of your estimate.

Do you offer a warranty?

We stand behind our workmanship, and materials carry manufacturer warranties. Ask us for the specific terms that apply to your project during your inspection.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in New Braunfels and serve the surrounding Hill Country, including Canyon Lake, Gruene, Seguin, San Marcos, Spring Branch, Bulverde, and Schertz.
